# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and productive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Give and receive feedback with care.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Serious incidents should be reported privately to the maintainers through the repository's published contact path.
