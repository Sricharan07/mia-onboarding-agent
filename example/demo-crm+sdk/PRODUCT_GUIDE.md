# Mia Demo CRM product guide

## Navigation

The primary navigation contains Default, CRM, Finance, Analytics, Productivity, E-commerce, Academy, and Logistics dashboards. CRM is available at `/dashboard/crm`. Finance is available at `/dashboard/finance`.

## CRM dashboard

The CRM dashboard helps sales teams review pipeline health, qualified lead flow, upcoming meetings, proposal progress, task reminders, and recent opportunities.

The Recent Opportunities table shows each account, owner, sales stage, health, amount, probability, close date, and next step. The Stage filter narrows the table to opportunities in selected sales pipeline stages. The Health filter narrows the table by health status. The search field filters visible opportunities by account, contact, owner, stage, or next step.

Opportunity stages are Proposal Sent, Discovery, Negotiation, and Qualified. Health values are On Track, Needs Review, At Risk, and On Hold.

## Draft opportunities

Create draft adds a reversible opportunity draft. A draft is not sent, published, or externally communicated. A draft starts in the Qualified stage and can be reviewed before it is used. Mia must ask for confirmation before creating or updating a draft.

Mia can update reversible opportunity fields after confirmation. Mia cannot delete opportunities, send proposals, publish records, approve requests, make payments, contact customers, or complete irreversible submissions.

## Finance dashboard

The Finance dashboard summarizes personal finances, including net worth, available cash, monthly spending, savings rate, recent transactions, and budget trends.

## Using Mia

Mia answers questions from the current interface and approved product knowledge. Mia can point to controls, highlight them, navigate within the product, fill reversible fields, and run reviewed product actions. The visible Mia cursor is separate from the user's physical pointer.

Text and voice share the same session and safety policy. Voice uses the Aoede voice. Hold Control+Space to talk with push-to-talk, or use the microphone control for open voice mode. Stop Mia cancels the current model request, speech, queued action, navigation animation, and pending confirmation.
